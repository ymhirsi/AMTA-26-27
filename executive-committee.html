The Executive Committee

AMTA is led by a volunteer board of music teachers who give their time and expertise to keep our community thriving.

Our officers are elected from among AMTA's active membership and serve staggered terms. Each role carries specific responsibilities — from programming and membership to financial oversight and our annual student auditions.
