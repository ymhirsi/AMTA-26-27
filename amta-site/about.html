About AMTA

A community of music teachers, university faculty, and music educators serving upper East Tennessee since 1972.

Our Mission

Drawn from Article II of our Constitution:

To promote professional and social relations between members; to provide programs, discussions, and activities of interest to private music teachers, college and university faculty, and others interested in music teaching; to raise the standards of music teaching, performance, and appreciation among teachers, institutions, and the general public in upper East Tennessee; and to encourage greater respect for the music teaching profession.

Our History

The Appalachian Music Teachers Association was founded in 1972 as a local affiliate of the Tennessee Music Teachers Association and the Music Teachers National Association.

What We Do

AMTA serves its members and the broader community through:

  • Monthly programs — conferences, masterclasses, and recitals featuring respected scholars and performers.
  • Annual student auditions — adjudicated performances at ETSU each spring, leading to the Honors Recital and TMTA State Competitions.
  • Professional fellowship — bringing teachers together for shared learning and support.
  • Affiliation with TMTA and MTNA — connecting our members and students to a national network.
Affiliations

Part of a Larger Community

TMTA

Tennessee Music Teachers Association

AMTA is a local affiliate of TMTA — the state association that organizes the Tennessee State Competitions and statewide professional development. Students who earn Superior ratings at our spring auditions advance to TMTA State.

MTNA

Music Teachers National Association

Through our TMTA affiliation, AMTA members are part of MTNA — the national professional organization for music teachers, providing certification, advocacy, and the annual MTNA national conference.
