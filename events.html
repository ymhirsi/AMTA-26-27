Lecture

Big Pieces for Small Hands

Dr. David Brickle, Associate Professor of Music, Milligan University

A lecture on navigating some of the difficulties faced by pianists with smaller hand-spans. Examples focus on challenges common in Romantic Era repertoire but with broad applicability. Fingering principles, redistribution, and other modifications will be discussed. Dr. Brickle completed his Master of Music and Doctor of Music degrees in piano performance, as well as the Specialized Studies Program in Pedagogy of Music Theory, at The Florida State University.

Milligan University · Seeger Chapel · Milligan, TN

Piano Masterclass with Dr. Chia-Ying Chan

Dr. Chia-Ying Chan, Piano Faculty, Saginaw Valley State University

A masterclass suitable for early intermediate to advanced pianists, offering fresh perspectives on standard performance repertoire leading up to the April AMTA Auditions. Open and free for all to observe. Teachers entering students must apply by February 21; a $25 registration fee is due upon confirmation of acceptance (check postmark February 25). Six students will be selected for 15-minute coaching sessions each, with memorized repertoire required.

Dr. Chan is an active educator, clinician, and adjudicator who has presented masterclasses throughout the United States and Taiwan and has offered webinar series for students and teachers in China.

AMTA Annual Auditions & Honors Recital

Open to students of AMTA member teachers

Our flagship student event. Students of all levels audition before adjudicators; those earning a Superior rating are invited to perform in the Honors Recital and advance to TMTA State Competitions. No musicianship tests are required this year. The Honors Recital is tentatively scheduled for 1:00 PM following the auditions; final timing depends on the number of entrants.

Registration fee is $25 per event. Registration deadline is a postmark no later than March 15, 2027 [CONFIRM YEAR — yearbook lists March 15, 2026; likely a carryover from prior year, verify before publishing]. AMTA members will receive an email with the registration application on or before February 20. For questions about repertoire selections or the audition process, contact Benjamin Caton or another AMTA officer.
