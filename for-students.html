For Students

Auditions, masterclasses, and opportunities to perform.

AMTA Annual Auditions

Each spring, students of AMTA member teachers gather at ETSU's Mathes Music Building for our annual auditions — a chance to perform prepared repertoire for adjudicators and potentially advance to TMTA State Competitions.

Next Auditions: Saturday, April 10, 2027 · 8:00 AM – 3:30 PM
Location: ETSU Mathes Music Building, Johnson City
Registration deadline: Postmark by Sunday, March 15, 2026.
Audition fee: $25 per event
Note: No musicianship tests are required this year.

March 6 Masterclass with Dr. Chia-Ying Chan

AMTA hosts an annual piano masterclass designed to help students prepare for the April Auditions. This year's masterclass is led by Dr. Chia-Ying Chan, an active educator, clinician, and adjudicator who has presented masterclasses throughout the United States and Taiwan and offered webinar series for students and teachers in China. She serves on the piano faculty at Saginaw Valley State University.

Date: Saturday, March 6, 2027
Presenter: Dr. Chia-Ying Chan, Saginaw Valley State University
Location: [Location TBD]
Level: Early intermediate to advanced pianists
Format: Six students selected for 15-minute coaching sessions each
Repertoire: Must be memorized
Observing: Open and free for all attendees

How to apply: Teachers entering students must apply by February 21, 2027. Upon confirmation of acceptance, a $25 registration fee is due, with check postmarked no later than February 25, 2027.
